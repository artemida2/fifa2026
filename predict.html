Feature Nº 05 · The Predictor

Call the whole tournament.

A three-step ritual — pick your group, pick your favorites, crown your champion.

Step 1 — pick the top two finishers in each of the 12 groups. Step 2 — pick the best third-placed teams to fill the Round of 32. Step 3 — advance winners all the way to the final. Your progress saves automatically in this browser.

Progress

Each group has its own click-counter that cycles 1ST 2ND 3RD 1ST… Tap any team to assign it the next rank (it steals that rank from whoever had it). Unassigned ranks stay empty — intentional.

Click a team in each match to advance them through the playoffs. Your champion appears below.
